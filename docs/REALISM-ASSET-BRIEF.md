# Next character quality target

The current model remains a generated MakeHuman character with locally authored simplified internal anatomy. The October 1 rendering revision improves presentation, but does not meet a photographic human-quality target. Test passes verify game behavior, not artistic realism.

A replacement should depict a Black adult with a complete continuous body, fitted opaque shorts, detailed hands and feet, realistic eyelids and eyes, modeled or supported hair, neutral lighting in its skin maps, and natural body proportions. Prefer a professionally sculpted or scanned character with diffuse, normal, roughness, eye and hair maps; a full skeletal rig; and facial/eyelid blend shapes. Preserve the user's Black main-character requirement.

Before choosing a commercial asset, verify the actual downloadable body topology, rig, maps, expressions, web-rendered appearance and license. A marketplace render alone does not prove runtime quality. The license must permit the intended distributed browser game; source-asset redistribution in a public repository and downloadable development package needs separate checking. No asset purchase has been authorized or made.

Integrate a selected model by aligning the six anatomy layers to its shape, replacing the current pose deformation with its rig, retaining part picking, and profiling the mobile build. Detailed medical internal anatomy needs its own accurate model assets; a realistic outer character does not automatically supply realistic organs or vessels.

Current revision: soft studio environment reflections, lower skin specular response, removal of hard self-shadow artifacts, fine close-cropped curl geometry, thinner brows, a larger body exhibit and a face close-up control. No claim of photorealism, motion capture or a clinical atlas is made.
